## Known risks
- Version-check functions are pure and do not touch the DB; the application
  layer must call them inside a DB transaction (read + check + write) or the
  optimistic-lock guarantee does not hold. This is documented in the module
  header but not yet enforced by any application-layer code, since no
  persistence layer exists yet in Phase 1.

## Decisions
- Modeled two complementary mechanisms per docs/architecture/concurrency-and-conflicts.md:
  optimistic version checks for mutable aggregates (TaskAssignment, Family,
  Reward, TaskTemplate) and state-machine gates / idempotency keys for
  pipeline replay safety (already implemented in P1-008).
- CONFLICT_SCENARIOS captures the 6 known races in the vertical slice as
  structured, testable fixtures rather than prose only, so future services
  regress-test against the documented resolution.
