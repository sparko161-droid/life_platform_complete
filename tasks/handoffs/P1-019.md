## Known risks
- Recorded as DISC-P1-019-1 (non-blocking): persisted aggregates carry only
  an optimistic-concurrency `version` counter, not a schema-shape version.
  Deliberately not retrofitted here -- no persistence layer exists yet
  (BLK-P1-007 open) to inform what shape that field actually needs to
  protect against, and it touches every aggregate-construction call site
  across three service files. Proposed to land with the persistence layer
  itself.
- `checkSchemaCompatibility` only detects field-presence/optionality
  breakage (removed field, optional→required, new required field without
  default). It does not detect type-narrowing within a field (e.g. an enum
  losing a member) -- documented as a known limitation in the module
  docstring and exercised as such in the tests, not silently claimed as
  exhaustive.
- `SURFACE_VERSION_STATUS` honestly records that 5 of 6 versioned surfaces
  have no independent version number yet (only `domain-contracts` does, via
  the existing `CONTRACT_VERSION`). This is Phase 1's documented minimum
  scope ("implement the ... mechanisms necessary to introduce them safely
  later"), not an omission -- fabricating version numbers for surfaces
  nothing tracks would be a false claim, not progress.

## Decisions
- `evaluateFeatureFlag` is pure and storage-agnostic (no flag-definition
  store) since no running service exists yet to read flags from at
  runtime -- the mechanism is delivered now, wiring it to a real store is
  an application-layer concern for whichever later task stands one up.
- `checkSchemaCompatibility` operates on zod's shape introspection
  (`.shape`, `.isOptional()`) directly rather than a bespoke schema IR, to
  stay generic over any `z.object(...)` in the repo without new
  dependencies.
