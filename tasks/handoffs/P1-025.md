## Decisions
- No ORM (confirmed in the BLK-P1-006-007 packet): hand-written
  repository methods, parameterized `pg` queries.
- `services/api` becomes ESM; the two migration files were renamed to
  `.cjs` (not left as `.js`) so they stay CommonJS regardless of the
  package's module type -- `.cjs`/`.mjs` always win over the package.json
  `"type"` field, which is what node-pg-migrate's own loader expects.
- `requireActiveParentMemberOrSystem` querying `parent_memberships`
  directly is what makes RT-003 (child self-approval) fail the same way
  as RT-002 (arbitrary outsider) -- no separate "is this actor a child"
  check was needed, since a child's id is never a row in that table.
- Ledger idempotency: application-layer lookup (via the same
  `taskCompletionRewardKey`/etc. helpers the domain layer uses) plus
  `INSERT ... ON CONFLICT (idempotency_key) DO NOTHING` as DB-level
  defense-in-depth against the race the lookup alone could in principle
  lose.
