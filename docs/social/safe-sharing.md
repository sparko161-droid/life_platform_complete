# Safe sharing

**Owner:** Safety Lead + Social Lead

A child or family success is shareable only through an explicit privacy policy.

## Shareable objects
Achievement, completed quest, streak, approved case, challenge result and selected media.

## Defaults
Money, private messages, precise location, school data and sensitive media are private.

## Flow
`PARENT_SELECTS → POLICY_CHECK → MODERATION → SHARE → AUDIT`.

## Reuse
A shared case can be converted into a template only after content moderation and removal of family-specific data.

## Acceptance
A parent can revoke sharing; future visibility stops and previously derived public-template data keeps only the minimum anonymized content required by policy.
