# Parent Social Loop

**Owner:** Social Lead
**Review:** Product, Safety, UX

## Loop

`Profile → interests → consented discovery → friendship → chat → shared case → family activity`.

## Rules

- Parent friendship is independent of child friendship.
- Suggestions use only approved profile attributes and existing trusted connections.
- No automatic exposure of phone, address, school or exact location.
- A shared child success becomes a shareable case only by parent action.
- Family-to-family activity requires explicit parent consent.

## Grouping

Future groups may be interest, activity or challenge based and must carry moderation policy.

## Acceptance

A parent can connect with another parent, communicate, share a case and later revoke the relationship without changing the child's historical progress.
