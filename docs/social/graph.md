# Social Graph

**Status:** Foundation
**Owner:** AI CTO
**Depends on:** MASTER_SPEC
**Related:** MASTER_SPEC


## Graphs
Parent Friendship, Child Friendship, Family Friendship and Groups are separate relationships.

## Consent
Child friendship activation requires parent-controlled permission. Parent friendship is an adult relationship; child access does not follow automatically.

## Discovery
No unrestricted child discovery. Suggestions can be based on approved family connections and shared interests.

## Sharing
Cases/achievements are opt-in. Money, private messages and sensitive media are hidden by default.

## Family friendship
The system can suggest family-level activities after repeated positive child interactions, but never expose private contact data automatically.
