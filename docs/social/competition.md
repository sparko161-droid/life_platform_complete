# Competition and Challenges

**Owner:** Social Lead
**Review:** Child Experience, Game, Safety, QA

## Modes

`PERSONAL_BEST`, `ONE_V_ONE`, `TEAM`, `COOPERATIVE`, `FAMILY`, `PROGRESS`.

## Fairness

- Competition is optional.
- Age and capability bands prevent inappropriate comparisons.
- Prefer progress and consistency metrics over raw totals.
- Private money, parent spending and sensitive family data are never public ranking inputs.
- Anti-spam and anti-cheat rules apply to score events.

## Lifecycle

`DRAFT → INVITED → ACTIVE → FINISHED → SETTLED`.

## Settlement

Rewards are derived from final trusted events, not client scores.

## Acceptance

A challenge can be cancelled, resumed or disputed without corrupting underlying child progress.
