# Parent Social and Family Matching

**Owner:** Social Lead
**Review:** Child Safety, Product, UX

## Parent social layer
Parents may discover other parents through approved friendship requests, shared interests, local/remote family activity preferences and participation in the same cases.

## Family bridge
A child friendship does not automatically create a parent friendship. A parent friendship does not automatically authorize child friendship. A family-level relationship requires explicit consent on both sides.

## Shared interests
Interest profiles are coarse and opt-in: games, sport, reading, travel, crafts, learning and similar activities. Exact location and private contact details are not exposed for matching.

## Case sharing
Parents can publish anonymized success cases and statistics. Published cases must exclude money, private messages, sensitive media and unnecessary child identifiers.

## Competition
Leaderboards use age bands and safe metrics. Real-money amounts are never public by default. Ranking may use XP, streak consistency, challenge completion or cooperative achievements.

## Family activities
The platform may suggest a safe family challenge after a positive relationship is established. Direct offline meeting is outside the product's trust boundary and requires user-controlled coordination.
