# Case: Parent Social → Family Friendship

**Actors:** Two parents, two children
**Domains:** Parent Social, Child Social, Messenger, Cases

1. Parent profiles approved interests.
2. Platform suggests a trusted connection.
3. Both parents accept parent friendship.
4. Parents may start a private chat.
5. A parent shares a child success case.
6. The other family applies the case to its child.
7. Repeated positive interactions may suggest a family activity.
8. Any family-level connection remains explicitly consented.

## Privacy

Child identity, money, private messages and exact location are not exposed by the social suggestion itself.
