# Learning and Development Profile

**Owner:** Learning Lead
**Review:** Product, AI Safety, Child Experience

## Purpose
Represent what a child is practicing, what they have demonstrated and which age-appropriate areas may need attention without presenting the system as a medical or psychological diagnosis.

## Evidence
Evidence may come from completed tasks, parent observations, learning sessions, exercise results and approved assessments. Every derived insight stores source, timestamp, confidence and methodology version.

## Areas
Reading, writing, mathematics, language, general knowledge, self-care, household responsibility, movement, communication, creativity and digital literacy.

## Age adaptation
Recommendations use configured age bands and development guidance. The platform must distinguish evidence-backed guidance from product heuristics.

## Parent control
Parents can disable categories, override recommendations and delete or correct observations.

## AI boundary
AI may summarize evidence and propose practice. It must not diagnose a child or make high-impact claims from sparse data.
