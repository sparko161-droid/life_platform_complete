# Product Principles

**Status:** Foundation
**Owner:** AI CTO
**Depends on:** MASTER_SPEC
**Related:** MASTER_SPEC


## Product promises
1. The child experiences a game, not a chore database.
2. Parents retain control over family rules and money.
3. Social features connect families, not expose children to open discovery.
4. Recommendations are optional; parent configuration is authoritative.
5. Learning is embedded into natural interactions rather than forced as a school layer.
6. Money is an optional reward channel, never the only motivator.

## UX principles
- Child: visual, short, positive, immediate feedback.
- Parent: fast setup, high information density, low daily administration cost.
- Admin: auditability, moderation, explainability.

## Trust principles
- No silent privilege escalation.
- No hidden state mutation by clients.
- No AI claim presented as professional diagnosis.
- No public child profile by default.
