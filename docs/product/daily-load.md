# Daily Load

**Owner:** Child Experience Lead
**Review:** Product, Learning, QA

## Purpose

Prevent the game from turning into an unlimited checklist or grind loop.

## Model

Daily load is composed of `mandatory`, `habit`, `optional`, `quest` and `challenge` work.

## Rules

- Age band and parent policy define recommended bounds.
- Tasks with repetitive low-value rewards may be capped.
- Missed optional tasks do not create debt.
- The system may recommend balancing domains but cannot force parent choices.
- Screen time and money cannot be the only completion motivator.

## Acceptance

A generated day can be previewed with load, estimated time and reward exposure before activation.
