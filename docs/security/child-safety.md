# Child Safety

**Status:** Foundation
**Owner:** AI CTO
**Depends on:** MASTER_SPEC
**Related:** MASTER_SPEC


## Default
Private child profiles. No unrestricted discovery. No public child contact data.

## Communication
Parent consent controls child friend/chat access. Reporting and blocking are always available.

## Moderation
Text, voice transcription and video metadata may be moderated using automated systems plus human review for escalations.

## Safety incidents
High-risk reports create a safety case with immutable audit trail and restricted access.

## Product language
Do not present developmental recommendations as diagnosis or professional assessment.
