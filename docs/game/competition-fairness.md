# Competition and Fairness

**Owner:** Game Design Lead
**Review:** Child Experience, Child Safety, QA

## Motivation
Competition is optional. Cooperative goals and personal-best goals are first-class alternatives.

## Metrics
Prefer XP, completed challenges, streak consistency, learning progress and team achievements. Never expose family money balances as a competitive score by default.

## Age fairness
Leaderboards and challenges are segmented by configured age bands and capability where necessary. Cross-age competition must use normalized goals rather than raw counts.

## Anti-abuse
Rate limits, anomaly detection and review prevent spam, artificial completion, collusion and unsafe challenge escalation.

## Social rewards
Messaging bonuses reward meaningful interaction with caps and quality signals. Sending repeated low-value messages must not farm XP.

## Parent control
Parents can disable competition, hide rankings and restrict challenges.
