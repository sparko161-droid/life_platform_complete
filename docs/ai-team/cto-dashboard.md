## Dashboard sections
- Delivery: active, blocked, review and completed tasks.
- Architecture: pending decisions, drift alerts, ADR proposals.
- Quality: flaky tests, regression risk, technical debt.
- Security: open findings, child-safety incidents, permission anomalies.
- Operations: deploys, incidents, queue lag, cost.

## Decision queue
Each item includes why the human must decide, options, recommendation and affected roadmap.

## Principle
Human Architect receives conclusions and evidence, not a full codebase dump.
